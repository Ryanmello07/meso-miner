## What 34.0-rc1 is

The v2026 engine transplant. The provider's connect generation moves from the v3.23 fork engine to the pinned v2026 engine (connect `c8d8cdf536a8`, the module the sn line pins), landed in five slices. Each slice adopts upstream files on the leaf rule, keeps the fork's behaviour behind adapters, and follows the apply plan in `scratch/v2026-engine-port/APPLY_PLAN.md`. The wire format does not move: `protocol/*.proto` and the reproduced `.pb.go` files stay as they are. v33.1 merges forward first and is part of this rc.

This is the engine generation the sn provider has been running in the field work on NY5. The rc exists to put the same generation behind the 3.23-fix provider and soak it there before the meso mirror follows.

## Planned content

### Slice 1 — Family dial + DoH family policy

The control-family policy and its listeners (`ControlIpFamilyPolicy`, `IpFamilyAuto`, `NetworkChanged`, `NetworkQualityChanged`) land as the shared base every later slice sits on. DoH family policy and the address-family dial preference move to the pinned engine's shape. The fork adapter keeps IPv4 as the primary control plane: a platform dial that fails through a proxy is never counted as a family strike, and IPv4 is never demoted; only IPv6 may narrow.

### Slice 2 — Egress, route manager, platform budget

The egress security policy, route manager and platform budget adoption from the pinned engine. Fork additions stay in `transport_fork_helpers.go` and the recorded adaptation tables, never silent edits to leaf files.

### Slice 3 — ICE resolver + routing classifier

The ICE resolver and the routing classifier move to the pinned engine, including the net-quality listener consumed by the transfer slice. Special-use ranges and IPv4-mapped IPv6 handling keep the fork's v33 behaviour.

### Slice 4 — Shared provider UDP socket lifecycle

MEMSTEADY direction 1: the provider's UDP socket lifecycle is shared rather than per-transport, bounding sockets and read goroutines on a box that runs many proxies. This is the slice the sn field work measured as the memory-growth source on saturated boxes.

### Slice 5 — Transfer layer: memory bounds, reliable lane, subprotocols

The transfer layer adopts the pinned engine's memory bounds and exact-admission budget core, the reliable lane provenance and per-route ack counting, the subprotocol registry, dispatch, query and send surface, and the opt-in retained accounting flags. Logical lanes (C4) are deliberately last and separate, and are not part of this rc.

## Ground rules observed

- **Inert adopt first.** A slice may land files nothing calls; new behaviour is enabled by a later reviewed commit.
- **Leaf rule.** A leaf file that is byte-identical to the pin stays byte-identical; every adaptation is recorded, never silent.
- **Fork-only files survive.** No slice deletes, renames or shadows a fork-only file.
- **No wire break.** No protocol or `.pb.go` change.
- **Commits.** Signed conventional commits, one per coherent unit.

## Not in this rc

- Logical lanes, and the strategy-sequential dial change, stay on their own branches.
- Stage B `--adopt-legacy-provider-key` remains design-only.
