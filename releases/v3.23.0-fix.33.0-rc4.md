# v3.23.0-fix.33.0-rc4

> [!NOTE]
> Fourth pre-release of the v33 line: rc3 plus the Bittensor mainnet operator surface and a provider hardening pass. It adds the operators, fleet, claim-daemon and wallet-hotkey command groups with the Bittensor operations docs, serves `/metrics` by default (never on a public interface), and lands the TLS, stream and p2p accounting together with the transport, DoH, WebRTC, DMCA and wallet-journal fixes. Not a stable release: it is invisible to `urnet-tools update`. Try it on one box first.

## Highlights

- **Operator surface for Bittensor.** The `provider operators` group (status, plan and apply), the `fleet` group (bind, revoke, status), the claim daemons (v1 and v2: durable queues, admission, restart reconciliation) and the wallet hotkey family (delegate, revoke, status) land together with the port.
- **`/metrics` on by default, never public.** A node whose operator never ran `metrics on` now has a listener: `/metrics` starts at provider startup, and a public bind is refused, so a shared `--network host` container cannot publish the per-proxy series. TLS handshakes are labelled by dial path and host class, and stream accounting covers kinds, lifetimes, reasons, open requests and p2p churn.
- **Transport and network bounds.** TLS resumes per dial path, dead identities back off longer and the pinned pool is shared; a pin is disabled only once its replacement is up; upgrade routes are named by identity, not a dialer pointer; and a stream sequence ends with its transport. DoH connections are bounded per origin, WebRTC samples live STUN servers per session with a bounded gather wait, and the proxy address pattern compiles once with bracketed IPv6 support.
- **DMCA flow-table cleanup.** Idle flow-table entries are reclaimed by a `FlowTtl` scan, with metrics for the flow table and the scan, and the recently-reclaimed ring is lock-guarded.
- **Settlement vault resolution.** Claim and claim-v2 resolve the settlement vault from its own field, with the contract fallback, so a claim no longer depends on the contract read succeeding.
- **Wallet consent journal lock.** Two concurrent `wallet set` runs can no longer lose an entry or sign twice: the journal's read-modify-write is locked and the second run waits, then replays the retained consent or refuses.
- **Hybrid PQ end-to-end test.** A loopback test has the provider side negotiate `X25519MLKEM768` and hold a live sealing cipher, with the PQ tracker counting the session; the negative case runs with encryption off.
- **Bittensor operations docs.** Daemon credentials, fleet plans and the operator layout are documented under `docs/bittensor`.
- **Shakedown evidence.** The shakedown asserts the multi-provider status contract rather than user labels, re-pins with the release's own tool, and captures the restart return code plus systemd/journal state when a restart check fails.

## Fixed

- Claim and claim-v2 settlement vault resolution, in both the v1 and v2 paths.
- DoH connections are bounded per origin, and expired association blocks are cleared.
- WebRTC gathers against live STUN servers, and the gather wait is bounded.
- The proxy address pattern is compiled once, and bracketed IPv6 hosts parse.
- Only `StreamOpen` frames count as stream opens, and the connect-failure counter states what it counts.
- A transport pin is disabled only once its replacement is up, upgrade routes are named by identity, and a stream sequence is joined to its transport's lifetime.
- The DMCA recently-reclaimed ring is guarded by a lock.
- The wallet consent journal's read-modify-write is locked.
- The durable fixture closure is kept out of non-unix builds.
- CI: the gofmt gate skips the vendored `third_party` tree.
- Test gates: seed-custody, lock serialization and durablevolume attribute-mutation tests skip where the platform cannot provide those primitives; Windows and FreeBSD gates are explicit.

## Known limits

- Same transfer-layer limits as rc3; the client send path for ICMP stays off.
- Prereleases are not offered by `urnet-tools update`.
